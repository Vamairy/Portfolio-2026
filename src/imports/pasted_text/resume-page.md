IMPORTANT EXPERIENCE DESIGN DIRECTION

Do not make this look like a standard résumé timeline.

Explore a more contemporary editorial layout.

For example, dates could live in a narrow column while role, company and impact occupy a larger content area.

Metrics can behave almost like graphic elements: large numbers with small contextual labels.

Use typography, whitespace and subtle lines rather than containers to establish hierarchy.

The most recent experience should have slightly more visual emphasis than older roles.

Use the portfolio gradient very selectively on important metrics, small details, hover states or typography.

Do not overuse gradients.
